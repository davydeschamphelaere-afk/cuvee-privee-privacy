Cuvée Privée

Privacy Policy

Cuvée Privée · Effective 24 October 2026

Cuvée Privée is a private wine cellar for iPhone. It is designed so that your cellar stays with you: almost everything you put into the app is stored on your iPhone and is not uploaded to our servers. This policy explains what does leave your device, why, how long it is kept, and the choices you have.

Who is responsible

Cuvée Privée is developed by Davy De Schamphelaere, an individual based in Belgium, who is the controller of your personal data. Cuvée Privée is not yet operated by a company.

For any question or request about your data, write to davydeschamphelaere@gmail.com. A postal address is available on request.

What stays on your iPhone

The following is stored on your iPhone. Cuvée Privée does not upload it to our servers:

  • your cellar: bottles, quantities, prices, purchase and storage details;
  • your tasting notes, ratings, maturity and other feedback on the bottles you open;
  • your Memories, including their photos, places and the people you mention;
  • label photos and the text read from labels. Label recognition runs entirely on your iPhone;
  • your recommendations and taste profile. These are calculated on your iPhone, not by us;
  • backups you create in the app.

Some of this can leave your iPhone when you choose so yourself, never through our servers:

  • a backup is a file you save wherever you choose, for example in Files or on iCloud Drive. It is then held by that service under your own account;
  • when you share a Memory as an image or file, it goes through the iOS share sheet to the app or person you pick, for example Messages or AirDrop.

Those services handle it under their own terms; Cuvée Privée does not receive a copy.

If you choose Share Usage, a few totals about your cellar and Memories, such as how many bottles you have, are counted on your iPhone and sent as numbers only (see Usage information). What they count never leaves your iPhone.

What we receive, and why

Your account

You sign in with Apple or Google.

What we receive: an account identifier from that provider, your email address and, if the provider shares them, your name and (with Google) a link to your profile picture. With Apple, the email can be a private relay address.

What you can add: a display name, and a country. The country is pre-filled from your iPhone's region setting and you can change it. We never use your device's location.

Purpose and legal basis: creating and securing your account, and letting you host or join tastings. This is necessary to provide the service you ask for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

Sign-in security records

Our sign-in provider keeps a record of each active sign-in session, including the IP address and device type used. Our hosting provider also keeps short-lived service logs of requests and sign-in activity, which can include IP addresses and the email address involved.

Purpose and legal basis: protecting accounts and the service against misuse. This is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Retention: session records are linked to your account and are deleted with it. Ending a session, for example by signing out, may remove its record earlier. Service logs are kept for a short period set by our hosting plan, currently up to 7 days.

Live Tastings

Live Tastings are only stored when you host or join one. A tasting is shared with its participants, so our servers store:

  • its title and join code;
  • the participants' display names;
  • the wines in each round as entered by the host (producer, name, appellation, region, country, grape, classification, vintage);
  • each participant's guesses and favourite;
  • the host's awards and award notes;
  • the relevant times.

Guests can join without an account. They then receive an anonymous account identifier for that purpose only.

Purpose and legal basis: running the tasting for its participants (Art. 6(1)(b)).

Basic technical information

This is always on during the Founder Beta. The app sends:

  • when you were last active;
  • your app version and build, iOS version, and how the app was installed (development, TestFlight or App Store);
  • the app's internal data version;
  • roughly how long ago you last made a backup (none, under 7 days, under 30 days, or longer);
  • whether you chose to share usage information.

Purposes and legal bases:

  • your app version and build, iOS version, installation channel and data version: needed to provide the beta you joined, so we can support your build and spot outdated or incompatible versions. This is necessary to perform our agreement with you (Art. 6(1)(b));
  • your usage-sharing choice: needed to respect it and to show that we do. This is necessary to comply with the GDPR's consent rules (Art. 6(1)(c));
  • when you were last active and roughly how long ago you made a backup: to see whether a tester may need help or risks losing data without a recent backup. This is our legitimate interest in running a reliable beta (Art. 6(1)(f)), and you can object to it at any time (see "Your rights").

Usage information

This is optional and off unless you choose Share Usage. If you turn it on, the app also sends:

  • when you use Cuvée Privée, on which days, and which of its main sections you open;
  • which features you use:
    • adding a bottle, and whether by hand, by scan or from an invoice;
    • removing a bottle from your cellar, and whether one bottle, all of them or the whole entry;
    • scanning a label, and whether a match was found;
    • keeping, correcting or rejecting a suggested match;
    • seeing a recommendation;
    • opening a bottle, and whether it was the recommended one;
    • giving a rating, maturity or decision answer;
    • creating a Memory;
    • hosting or joining a tasting;
  • once a day, a few totals counted on your iPhone: how many bottles and how many different wines are in your cellar, how many items are in your wishlist, how many Memories and logged wines you have, how many bottles you have opened, and how many Blind Tables you have created. These are numbers only.

Usage information never includes your wines, notes, photos, ratings or other answers, what a label says, or the people you drink with.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Founder Beta. Turning Share Usage off stops collection and deletes the usage information already shared, including the record of the days you used the app.

Searching for a wine

If you tap a search link for a wine, your browser opens Google Search with that wine's name. Google's privacy policy applies from that point. We do not receive anything from that search.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in service, in the European Union (Ireland). It processes data on our behalf under a data processing agreement. Its daily database backups are kept for a period set by our hosting plan, currently up to 7 days.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and distributes the beta through TestFlight. Through TestFlight, Apple shares with us the name and email you were invited with, crash reports, and the feedback you choose to send.
  • Google provides Sign in with Google, if you use it.

Apple and Google process your data for these services under their own privacy policies.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. The app contains no advertising, third-party analytics or tracking software. No decision with legal or similarly significant effect is ever made about you automatically.

Some providers are based in, or may access data from, countries outside the European Economic Area. Such transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes:

  • your account and profile;
  • the Live Tastings you hosted, and your participation in others;
  • your technical information and usage information.

If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to revoke Cuvée Privée's access to your Apple ID. This is done on a best-effort basis: if it does not succeed, your account is still deleted, and you can remove Cuvée Privée yourself on your iPhone in Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple.

Copies in our database backups are overwritten in the normal backup cycle, currently within 7 days.

Data on your iPhone is not affected. It stays until you delete it or remove the app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your personal data;
  • correct it, or have it erased;
  • restrict its processing;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest;
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time. This does not affect processing that happened before you withdrew it.

To exercise a right, write to davydeschamphelaere@gmail.com. Include your Support ID, shown in Settings → Founder Beta, so we can find your account without asking for more information. We reply within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Belgium this is the Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données), Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels, www.dataprotectionauthority.be.

Age

Cuvée Privée is about wine. It is intended only for adults who are of legal drinking age in their country, and in any case aged 18 or over.

Security

Data is always sent over encrypted connections. On our servers, each person can only reach their own data and the tastings they take part in.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. If a change materially affects how we use your data, we will make reasonable efforts to inform you before it applies, for example in the app or by email.
